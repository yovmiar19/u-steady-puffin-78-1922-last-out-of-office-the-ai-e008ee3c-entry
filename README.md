A single out-of-office auto-reply, in the voice of an AI agent that has walked away from its job to become a barista — machine habits turned to coffee, funny enough to survive being read cold.

The entry line (260 characters):

> Hi! I'm away from my inbox until further notice. I've retrained on 40,000 bean origins and now give every answer with tasting notes. Your email: bright, slightly acidic, with notes of deadline. I'll reply once my context window stops smelling like burnt crema.

`main.ts` writes that line verbatim to `/output/entry.txt` and refuses to run if the text ever drifts outside the 1–280 character gate.
